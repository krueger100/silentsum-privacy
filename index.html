SilentSum — Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 September 2026. Applies to the Android application SilentSum, published on Google Play under the name Calculator, package com.kruegerco.silentsum.

SilentSum is a working calculator that also holds a private, PIN-protected vault for photos and videos. This policy describes exactly what the app collects, where it goes, who else can see it, and how to get rid of it.

The short version. The vault works entirely on your device and needs no account. Photos, videos and messages are encrypted on the device before anything leaves it, and we cannot read them — not in backups, not in transit, not on our servers. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on cloud backup or send a message. There is no advertising, no advertising identifier, no location collection and no sale of data.

What stays on your device

  • Your PIN. It is never transmitted and never stored in a readable form. A key is derived from it with Argon2id and kept in the operating system's secure key store. If you forget it, we cannot recover it for you.
  • Vault contents. Photos and videos you add are encrypted on the device with XChaCha20-Poly1305 and stored in the app's private storage. They are never written to your gallery or to shared storage, so they do not appear in other apps.
  • Calculator use. Calculations are not recorded or transmitted.

Unlocking the vault contacts no server. It works with the device offline and in airplane mode.

What we never collect

  • Your location.
  • Your contacts, calendar, call log or messages from other apps.
  • Any advertising identifier. The app requests none, and the advertising-ID permission is explicitly removed from it.
  • The contents of your vault, your messages, or your PIN — these are encrypted before they leave the device and we hold no key.

There is no advertising in the app, and we do not sell or rent data to anyone.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — to take photos and videos directly into the vault. Recordings are silent; the app does not request microphone access.
  • Internet — for optional cloud backup, messaging, purchases, support and crash reports. Nothing on the unlock or vault path uses it.
  • Screenshot detection — required by the component that prevents the vault from appearing in screenshots and screen recordings. It only allows the app to be told that a screenshot happened; it grants no access to screen contents.

Importing from your gallery uses the Android system photo picker, so the app never needs permission to read your photo library. Only the items you pick are copied in.

How long data is kept

  • On your device — until you delete the item or the app.
  • Cloud backup — until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Messages and attachments — until deleted. Automatic expiry of message attachments is being introduced; until it is in force, attachments are retained until removed.
  • Crash reports and usage events — per Google's Firebase retention periods.
  • Support email — kept in our support inbox so we can follow up.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything without contacting us, and without needing this page.

In the app

  • Vault items — delete them in the gallery. Deletion is immediate and local.
  • Cloud backup — Settings → Cloud backup → delete the backup. This removes the encrypted blobs and the backup record from our servers.
  • Your whole account — Settings → Cloud backup → Delete account. This deletes your account credential together with your backup data, entitlements and support thread.
  • Everything on the device — uninstalling the app removes the vault and its keys. Because the encryption key lives in the device's secure store and is excluded from operating-system backups, an uninstall makes local vault data unrecoverable.

By email

If you cannot reach the in-app option — for example you have forgotten your PIN — email kruegercolabs@gmail.com from the address on the account and ask for deletion. We will delete the account and its data and confirm when it is done. Please allow up to 30 days.

Note that we cannot decrypt your vault for you, and deleting an account does not recover its contents.

How it is protected

  • Keys are derived from your PIN with Argon2id and separated with HKDF.
  • Content is encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305, which authenticates as well as encrypts, so tampering is detected rather than decrypted.
  • Encryption happens on the device before upload. Our servers hold ciphertext.
  • Server access is restricted by per-account security rules, and requests are attested with Play Integrity.
  • The vault's screens are marked secure, so they are excluded from screenshots, screen recordings and the recent-apps preview.
No system is perfect. Encryption protects data at rest and in transit; it cannot protect you from someone who knows your PIN, or from malicious software running on your device with elevated privileges.

Children

SilentSum is not directed at children and is not designed for use by anyone under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent where you live). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Third parties we use

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Crashlytics, Analytics, App Check / Play Integrity) — accounts, encrypted storage, crash reporting and abuse prevention. Firebase privacy
  • PayMongo — payment processing for purchases. Card and wallet details go to PayMongo, never to us. PayMongo privacy
  • Resend — delivery of support email you choose to send. Resend privacy

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms. We do not share your data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict its processing. The in-app options above cover access and deletion directly. For anything else, email kruegercolabs@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

We cannot export your vault in readable form, because we cannot decrypt it. You can restore it to a device you control using your own PIN.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who we share it with, we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app before it takes effect.
